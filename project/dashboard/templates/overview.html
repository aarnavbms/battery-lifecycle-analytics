Eight batteries, from first charge to recycling

Real lithium-ion cells, aged in the lab until they failed. The AI reads their health from charging data, predicts what is left, decides each one's second life, and signs every step into a Digital Battery Passport.

  • First life, 80% and above
  • Second life, 60 to 80%
  • Recycling, below 60%
