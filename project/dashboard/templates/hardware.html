Roadmap

The software is ready for each phase; only the hardware changes.

  1. Bench logger, one 18650 cellESP32, INA219 current sensor, DS18B20 temperature probe, TP4056 charger, MOSFET load. About INR 1,500. Firmware is in hardware/firmware.
  2. Real battery management systemsRead pack voltage, current, temperature and the BMS's own SOH over CAN bus or Modbus, mapped to the same JSON protocol.
  3. Fleet and passport registryMQTT from every pack to a time-series database, this pipeline as a scheduled job, and each passport's chain head anchored in the battery passport registry.

Safety rules built into the firmware

  • Hard stop above 4.25 V, below 2.50 V, or above 55 °C: charger and load both switched off.
  • Charger and load are never on together; the firmware switches both off before changing state.
  • Fuse on the cell's positive lead; protected cells or a protection board only.
  • Cycle inside a fire-safe container; never charge a swollen, dented or hot cell.
