The passport network

Every signed passport event is an interaction between an actor and a battery. Read together, the events form a network: who hands batteries to whom, and which lifecycle steps follow which. Network measures show who the passport system depends on most.

    How to read the measures

    In and out

    Weighted degree. In counts hand-offs an actor receives; out counts hand-offs it passes on.

    Betweenness

    Share of shortest paths between other actors that pass through this one. High means it is a broker: remove it and others are cut off.

    PageRank

    Importance from incoming hand-offs, weighted by how important the senders are.

    Density and reciprocity

    Density is the share of possible links that exist. Reciprocity is the share of links that also run the other way.

    This network is small because the study has nine batteries and six registered actors. The method is the same at any scale: it is computed from the signed events with NetworkX, so new passports and actors extend it automatically.
